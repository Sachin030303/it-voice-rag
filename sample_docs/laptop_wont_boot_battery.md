# Laptop Will Not Power On or Charge

## No power at all
Remove the charger, hold the power button for 30 seconds, reconnect the charger and try again. Test with a known-good charger and wall outlet.

## Powers on but no display
Connect an external monitor. If it works, the internal display or cable is faulty. Press Ctrl+Shift+Win+B on Windows to restart the graphics driver.

## Battery not charging
Check battery health in the BIOS or manufacturer tool. Update BIOS and chipset drivers. If the battery is swollen, stop using it and contact IT immediately.
